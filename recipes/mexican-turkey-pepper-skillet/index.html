Mexican Turkey and Pepper Skillet

Makes 2 servings. About 550-600 kcal, 70-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
99% lean ground turkey 1½ lb (680 g)
bell peppers, sliced 2
zucchini, diced 2 medium
yellow onion, diced 1 small
canned diced tomatoes, drained 1 cup
olive oil 2 tsp
cumin 2 tsp
smoked paprika 2 tsp
oregano 1 tsp
garlic cloves 2
juice of 1 lime
romaine 4 cups
avocado ½
salt and pepper

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss zucchini with half the oil and roast 15–20 minutes.
  2. Heat remaining oil in skillet. Brown turkey, then add onion and peppers; cook 5–7 minutes. Add garlic, spices and tomatoes; cook until turkey reaches 165°F.
  3. Finish with lime. Divide turkey and zucchini into two portions; serve each over 2 cups romaine with ¼ avocado.
