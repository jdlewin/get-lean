Smoky Chicken Taco Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breastCoat with the spices and sear or grill to 165F; rest and slice.
4 cups chopped romaine + 2 cups baby spinachDivide into two bowls as the base.
1 cup chopped tomatoesSplit between the two bowls.
1 medium avocadoDice and divide evenly between servings.
40 g shredded cheddarDivide evenly; it drives much of the fat, so measure.
1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurtMix with salsa and lime into the dressing.
1/4 cup salsa, plus extraMix into the dressing; serve extra on the side.
Juice of 1 limeSqueeze into the dressing; thin with water if needed.
2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt and pepperRub over the chicken before cooking.

Process

  1. Spice and cook the chicken to 165F; rest and slice.
  2. Stir yogurt, salsa, and lime into a dressing.
  3. Bowl the greens and tomatoes; add chicken, avocado, and cheddar.
  4. Serve with dressing and extra salsa; keep dressing separate if prepping ahead.

Note: Avocado and cheddar portions drive the calories — divide them evenly.
