Tex-Mex Chicken Skillet (dinner)

Makes 2 servings. About 650-800 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast — Dice, season with the spices, and sear in batches to 165F.
  • 2 bell peppers — Slice and cook 5-7 minutes with the onion until softened.
  • 1 medium yellow onion — Slice and cook with the peppers.
  • 1 medium avocado — Dice and divide evenly between servings; measure.
  • 40 g shredded cheddar — Divide evenly; measure.
  • 4 cups shredded romaine — Serve the skillet over it as the base.
  • 1/2 cup salsa (mild) — Stir into the skillet with lime and simmer 1-2 minutes.
  • 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt — Thin with lime juice and water into a crema for topping.
  • 1 tsp olive oil — Heat for searing the chicken; measure.
  • 2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + lime juice — Season the chicken; finish the skillet with salsa and lime.

Process

  1. Season diced chicken with the spices.
  2. Sear in a single layer (batches if needed) to 165F.
  3. Add peppers and onion 5-7 minutes; stir in salsa and lime, simmer 1-2 minutes.
  4. Serve over romaine; top with avocado, cheddar, and yogurt crema.

Note: Keep the salsa mild; measure avocado and cheese and split evenly.
