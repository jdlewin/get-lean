Miso-Ginger Salmon with Sesame Broccoli

Makes 2 servings. About 650-750 kcal, 50-60 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
salmon 1½ lb
broccoli florets 1½ lb
white miso 1 tbsp
low-sodium soy 1 tbsp
rice vinegar 1 tbsp
grated ginger 1 tbsp
garlic cloves 2
sesame oil 2 tsp
sesame seeds 1 tsp
honey 1 tsp

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss broccoli with 1 tsp sesame oil and roast 18–22 minutes.
  2. Whisk miso, soy, vinegar, ginger, garlic, remaining oil and honey. Brush over salmon on a lined tray.
  3. Bake salmon 10–15 minutes depending on thickness, until safely cooked and flaky. Divide salmon and broccoli evenly; sprinkle sesame seeds.
