Lemon-Rosemary Chicken & Spinach (dinner)

Makes 2 servings. About 600-750 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of the plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast — Season with rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper; sear to 165F and rest.
  • 1 medium cauliflower — Cut into florets, toss with half the oil and rosemary, and roast at 425F for 25-35 minutes.
  • 10 oz baby spinach — Wilt in a separate pan with garlic, adding by handfuls.
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, divided — Half for the cauliflower roast, half for the spinach pan.
  • 1 tbsp butter — Melt in the chicken skillet for the sauce.
  • 4 minced garlic cloves, divided — Half in the pan sauce (30 seconds), half in the spinach pan.
  • 1 lemon, juiced and zested — Simmer juice and zest into the pan sauce.
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken stock — Add to the skillet and simmer 2-3 minutes, scraping up browned bits.
  • 1 tsp dried (or 1 tbsp fresh) rosemary + thyme, salt, pepper — Season the chicken and cauliflower.

Process

  1. Roast cauliflower at 425F, 25-35 minutes.
  2. Sear seasoned chicken to 165F; rest on a plate.
  3. Cook half the garlic 30 seconds in butter; add stock, lemon juice, and zest; simmer 2-3 minutes.
  4. Wilt spinach with remaining oil and garlic; serve chicken with pan sauce, spinach, and cauliflower.

Note: Spoon the lemon sauce over the chicken, not the vegetables, to keep portions consistent.
