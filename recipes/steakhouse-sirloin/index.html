Steakhouse Sirloin & Veg

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 65-80 g protein, 20-35 g carbs, 40-55 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb lean sirloin steaks, trimmed
  • 1 lb asparagus
  • 1 medium cauliflower
  • 4 cups arugula
  • 1 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • Optional 1 oz blue cheese total
  • 1 lemon + 1 tsp Dijon + 1 garlic clove + salt and pepper

Process

  1. Cut cauliflower into florets; toss with half the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast at 425F for 25-35 minutes.
  2. Season steaks with salt and pepper; sear or grill to preference. Rest at least 5-10 minutes, then slice.
  3. Trim woody ends from asparagus; roast or pan-sear until tender-crisp. Mince garlic; toss arugula with lemon juice, Dijon, garlic, and remaining oil.
  4. Finish rested steaks with butter. Plate with vegetables, splitting blue cheese evenly if using.

Note: Cut, trimming, and butter swing the calories — skip the blue cheese for a lighter plate.
