Greek Lemon-Oregano Chicken Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 70-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
boneless, skinless chicken breast 1½ lb (680 g)
chopped romaine or mixed greens 6 cups
baby spinach 2 packed cups
cucumber, diced 1
cherry tomatoes, halved 1 pint
feta, crumbled 40 g
olive oil 2 tbsp
Juice of 1 lemon
Dijon mustard 1 tsp
garlic cloves, minced 2
dried oregano 2 tsp
Salt and black pepper

Shopping list

Process

  1. Season the chicken with oregano, half the garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken until browned and its thickest part reaches 165°F / 74°C. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
  3. Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, Dijon, remaining garlic, and a little black pepper to make the dressing.
  4. Divide the greens, spinach, cucumber, and tomatoes between two containers.
  5. Top with sliced chicken and divide the feta evenly. Store the dressing separately and add it immediately before eating.

Note: If preparing both portions in advance, keep the greens dry and the dressing separate so the salad stays crisp.
