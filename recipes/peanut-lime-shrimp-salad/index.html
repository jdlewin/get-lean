Peanut-Lime Shrimp Salad (lunch)

Makes 2 servings. About 500-600 kcal, 60-70 g protein per serving. Part of operation-get-lean.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb peeled, deveined shrimp — Thaw, pat dry, and sear 1-2 minutes per side until opaque.
  • 4 cups thinly sliced cabbage — Toss with most of the dressing as the slaw base.
  • 1 cucumber — Cut into matchsticks and toss with the slaw.
  • 4 cups baby spinach — Toss with the slaw.
  • 2 tbsp natural peanut butter — Whisk into the dressing; measure, don't free-pour.
  • 2 tsp sesame oil — Heat in the skillet for searing the shrimp.
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce — Whisk into the dressing.
  • Juice of 2 limes — Whisk into the dressing.
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 minced garlic cloves + 1 tsp grated ginger — Whisk into the dressing; thin with water a teaspoon at a time.
  • Optional scallions + sesame seeds — Slice/sprinkle over the finished bowls.

Process

  1. Whisk PB, soy, lime, vinegar, garlic, and ginger; thin with water until pourable.
  2. Sear shrimp in hot sesame oil, 1-2 minutes per side.
  3. Toss cabbage, cucumber, and spinach with most of the dressing; top with shrimp.
  4. Finish with remaining dressing and scallions; refrigerate components separately if prepping ahead.

Note: Peanut butter is calorie-dense — measure it. Mild, tangy sauce, not spicy.
