Tuna, Egg and Cucumber Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 450-550 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
tuna in water, drained 3 cans
aim for about 15 oz total drained weight
eggs 4 large
cucumber, diced 1
celery stalks, finely sliced 2
romaine or mixed greens 4 cups
plain nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
Dijon mustard 2 tsp
Juice of ½ lemon
dried dill or 1 tbsp chopped fresh dill 1 tsp
Salt and black pepper

Process

  1. Hard-boil the eggs to your preferred doneness, then cool, peel, and chop.
  2. Mix the drained tuna with the Greek yogurt, Dijon, lemon juice, dill, salt, and pepper.
  3. Fold in the cucumber, celery, and chopped eggs.
  4. Serve half the tuna mixture over half the greens. Refrigerate the second portion promptly.

Note: This is intentionally a substantial tuna portion to help the Sunday protein total. If you use tuna packed in oil, the calories will be higher.
