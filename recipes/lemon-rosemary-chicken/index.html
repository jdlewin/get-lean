Lemon-Rosemary Chicken & Spinach

Makes 2 servings. About 600-750 kcal, 65-80 g protein, 20-35 g carbs, 20-30 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Chicken breast, boneless skinless 1.5 lb
Cauliflower 1 medium
Baby spinach 10 oz
Olive oil, divided 1 tbsp
Butter 1 tbsp
Garlic cloves, minced, divided 4
Lemon, juiced and zested 1
Chicken stock, low-sodium 1/2 cup
Rosemary 1 tsp dried (or 1 tbsp fresh)
Thyme, salt, pepper to taste

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425F. Cut the cauliflower into florets; toss with half the oil and rosemary and roast 25-35 minutes.
  2. Season the chicken with rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper; sear to 165F and rest on a plate.
  3. Melt the butter in the chicken skillet; cook half the garlic 30 seconds. Add the stock, lemon juice, and zest; simmer 2-3 minutes, scraping up the browned bits.
  4. Wilt the spinach in a separate pan with the remaining oil and garlic, adding by handfuls. Serve the chicken with pan sauce, spinach, and cauliflower.

Note: Spoon the lemon sauce over the chicken, not the vegetables, to keep portions consistent.
