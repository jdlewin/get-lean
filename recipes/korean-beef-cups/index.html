Korean Beef Lettuce Cups

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 70-85 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

1.5 lb 93% lean ground beefBrown in a skillet, breaking it up, and drain excess fat.
4 large eggsCook to preference (jammy: simmer about 7 minutes, then ice-water bath) and halve.
1 large head butter lettuce or romaineSeparate leaves for cups.
1 lb broccoli floretsSteam or roast until tender-crisp.
1 cucumberSlice for the cups.
2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauceStir into the beef with vinegar and sesame oil; taste before adding more.
1 tsp sesame oilStir into the beef; measure.
3 minced garlic cloves + 1 tbsp grated gingerCook 30 seconds in the beef after browning.
1 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 sliced scallionsStir vinegar into the beef; top with scallions.
Optional mild kimchi, 2-4 tbspServe on the side.

Process

  1. Cook broccoli; cook eggs to preference and halve.
  2. Brown beef and drain; add garlic and ginger 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in soy, vinegar, and sesame oil (splash of water if dry); taste first.
  4. Serve beef in lettuce leaves with cucumber, broccoli, and halved eggs; top with scallions and optional kimchi.

Note: Kimchi adds tang and sodium, not protein — keep it optional and mild.
