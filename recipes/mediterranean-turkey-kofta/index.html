Mediterranean Turkey Kofta with Yogurt Sauce

Makes 2 servings. About 550-650 kcal, 70-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
99% lean ground turkey 1½ lb
onion grated ¼ small
garlic cloves 2
cumin 2 tsp
coriander 1 tsp
smoked paprika 1 tsp
parsley ¼ cup
olive oil 1 tbsp
nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
cucumber grated and squeezed dry ½
lemon juice 1 tbsp
dill 1 tsp
additional cucumber 1
tomatoes 2
salt and pepper

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Mix turkey, onion, garlic, spices, parsley, salt and pepper; form 8 oval kofta.
  2. Brush with measured oil and bake 15–20 minutes, until 165°F internally.
  3. Mix yogurt, grated cucumber, lemon and dill. Serve 4 kofta per person with half the cucumber-tomato salad and half the sauce.
