How the cooking works

Twice a week, and each cook only covers the next 2–3 days. That is the fix from the 2026-11-11 test cook (4 hours, 1 future lunch, no veg ready, fridge and containers maxed out): never cook the whole week at once.

  • Sunday cook → Mon–Wed. Roast veg, cook lunch proteins, bottle dressings. Fridge only what gets eaten in 3–4 days; freeze the rest.
  • Wednesday refresh → Thu–Sun. Fresh greens and cucumber, cook the shrimp lunches, move Fri–Sun boxes from freezer to fridge, roast more veg if the first batch is gone.
  • Fridge rule. Dressings, wet toppings, and greens stay separate till serving. Fish stays frozen till the night before, then thaws in the fridge.
  • Containers. Quart delis for prepped ingredients, 4oz jars for sauces and dressings. Pyrex with matching lids for finished portions — audit lids before the Sunday cook. See Decisions in voice-2026-11-11-19-51-00-Reviewing-Operation-Get-Lean-cooking-wor.

Keep heat mild; no mushrooms, green beans, cauliflower mash/rice. Measure hummus, peanut butter, nuts, oils, cheese.

Cook days

About 2 hours per cook, most of it hands-off. Quantities below are the full 2-serving batch as written in each linked recipe.

Sunday cook → Mon–Wed (about 2 hours)

  1. Set up (15 min). Clear the counters, heat the oven to 425°F. Wash all produce. Raw meat gets its own board, away from salad greens.
  2. Roast veg (35–45 min, mostly hands-off). Cauliflower and Brussels for Mon–Tue, Garlic-Parmesan Chicken & Veg veg method: florets and halved sprouts tossed with olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper at 425°F for 25–35 minutes, turning once. Two trays so nothing steams. Wash and dry spinach, arugula, and romaine while the oven runs; store greens with a paper towel in sealed boxes.
  3. Lunch proteins (30–45 min). Gochujang Beef, Cabbage & Spinach (Monday lunch, full batch): brown 1.5 lb beef, cook minced garlic and grated ginger 30 seconds in the fat, stir in soy, measured gochujang, and vinegar; cabbage 4–6 minutes, spinach wilted last. Fridge it fast — fry the eggs fresh at serving. Mediterranean chicken (Mediterranean Chicken Salad): oregano/salt/pepper to 165°F, rest, slice. Smoky taco chicken (Smoky Chicken Taco Salad): cumin/paprika/garlic powder to 165°F, rest, slice — seasoned on its own so it tastes different from the Mediterranean batch.
  4. Dressings into small jars. Peanut-lime (Peanut-Lime Shrimp Salad): peanut butter, soy, lime, vinegar, garlic, ginger, thinned pourable. Taco: yogurt stirred with salsa and lime.
  5. Portion and pack (15 min). Split every recipe in two. Fridge what gets eaten in 3–4 days; freeze the rest. Shrimp waits till Wednesday — sear thawed, patted-dry shrimp in hot sesame oil 1–2 minutes per side — or freeze it right after it cools.

Wednesday refresh → Thu–Sun (15–25 min)

Fresh greens and cucumber. Cook the shrimp lunches. Move Fri–Sun boxes from freezer to fridge. Roast more veg if the first batch is gone.
