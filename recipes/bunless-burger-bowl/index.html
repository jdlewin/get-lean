The Kitchen / Dinners

Bunless Burger Bowl with Potato Wedges

Makes 2 servings. About 800-1,000 kcal, 50-65 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
93% lean ground beef 1½ lb (680 g)
baby potatoes, cut into wedges 1 lb (454 g)
chopped romaine 6 cups
tomato, sliced 1 large
red onion, thinly sliced ½ small
shredded cheddar 40 g
olive oil 1 tbsp
plain nonfat Greek yogurt ½ cup
yellow mustard 1 tbsp
pickle relish, optional 1 tbsp
Salt, black pepper, garlic powder, and paprika

Shopping list

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Toss the potato wedges with the olive oil, salt, pepper, garlic powder, and paprika.
  2. Spread the wedges on a baking tray without crowding them. Roast for 30–40 minutes, turning halfway through.
  3. Season the ground beef with salt and pepper. Form into patties or cook as crumbled beef in a skillet until thoroughly cooked.
  4. Mix the Greek yogurt, mustard, and optional relish to make a burger-style sauce.
  5. Divide the romaine, tomato, and onion between two bowls. Add the beef, cheddar, and roasted potatoes.
  6. Finish with the yogurt sauce.

Note: This is the planned flex meal, not an open-ended cheat meal. Keep the measured portions, skip the bun and fries, and enjoy it without turning it into a weekend-long detour.
