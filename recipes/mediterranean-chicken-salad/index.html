Mediterranean Chicken Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 550-650 kcal, 65-75 g protein, 15-30 g carbs, 18-25 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Chicken breast, boneless skinless 1.5 lb
Arugula 4 cups
Baby spinach 4 cups
Cucumber 1 medium
Apple, diced (optional) 1 small
Pecans 20 g
Olive oil 1 tbsp
Dijon mustard 2 tsp
Lemon juice (or red-wine vinegar) Juice of 1 lemon (or 2 tbsp red-wine vinegar)
Garlic clove 1
Oregano, salt, pepper to taste

Process

  1. Season the chicken with oregano, salt, and pepper; grill or sear to 165F. Rest, then slice.
  2. Grate the garlic fine; whisk with the oil, Dijon, lemon, and pepper, thinning with water if needed.
  3. Divide the greens between two containers as the salad base; box with the chopped cucumber, sliced chicken, and chopped pecans. Keep the dressing separate.
  4. Dice the apple and add just before eating so it stays fresh.

Note: Omit the apple to keep carbs lower — lemon, Dijon, and pecans carry the flavor.
