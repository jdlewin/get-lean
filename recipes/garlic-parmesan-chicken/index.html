Garlic-Parmesan Chicken & Veg

Makes 2 servings. About 650-800 kcal, 65-80 g protein, 25-40 g carbs, 25-35 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Chicken breast, boneless skinless 1.5 lb
Cauliflower 1 medium
Brussels sprouts 1 lb
Parmesan, finely grated 30 g
Olive oil 1 tbsp
Butter 1 tbsp
Garlic cloves 4
Chicken stock, low-sodium 1/2 cup
Lemon juice Juice of 1/2 lemon
Italian seasoning, salt, pepper to taste

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425F. Cut the cauliflower into florets and halve the Brussels sprouts; toss with the olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Roast 25-35 minutes, turning once, until browned at the edges.
  2. Season the chicken with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Sear in a hot pan to 165F, then remove to a plate to rest.
  3. Melt the butter in the chicken pan. Mince the garlic and cook about 30 seconds until fragrant; pour in the stock and lemon juice, scraping up the browned bits.
  4. Stir in the Parmesan until the sauce comes together; return the chicken to coat. Plate with the roasted vegetables.

Note: Roast the vegetables until browned — flavor without extra sauce.
