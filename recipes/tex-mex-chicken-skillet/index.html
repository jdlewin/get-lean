Tex-Mex Chicken Skillet

Makes 2 servings. About 650-800 kcal, 65-80 g protein, 25-40 g carbs, 20-35 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Chicken breast, boneless skinless 1.5 lb
Bell peppers 2
Yellow onion 1 medium
Avocado 1 medium
Cheddar, shredded 40 g
Romaine, shredded 4 cups
Salsa, mild 1/2 cup
Greek yogurt, plain nonfat 1/2 cup
Olive oil 1 tsp
Cumin 2 tsp
Smoked paprika 1 tsp
Garlic powder 1 tsp
Lime juice 1 lime, to finish

Process

  1. Dice the chicken and season with the cumin, paprika, and garlic powder.
  2. Heat the oil; sear the chicken in a single layer — in batches if needed — to 165F.
  3. Slice the peppers and onion; cook 5-7 minutes until softened. Stir in the mild salsa and lime; simmer 1-2 minutes.
  4. Thin the yogurt with lime juice and water into a crema. Serve the skillet over the romaine; top with diced avocado, cheddar, and crema, dividing evenly.

Note: Keep the salsa mild. Measure the avocado and cheese and split them evenly.
