Smoky Chicken Taco Salad (lunch)

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of operation-get-lean.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast — Coat with the spices and sear or grill to 165F; rest and slice.
  • 4 cups chopped romaine + 2 cups baby spinach — Divide into two bowls as the base.
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes — Split between the two bowls.
  • 1 medium avocado — Dice and divide evenly between servings.
  • 40 g shredded cheddar — Divide evenly; it drives much of the fat, so measure.
  • 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt — Mix with salsa and lime into the dressing.
  • 1/4 cup salsa, plus extra — Mix into the dressing; serve extra on the side.
  • Juice of 1 lime — Squeeze into the dressing; thin with water if needed.
  • 2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt and pepper — Rub over the chicken before cooking.

Process

  1. Spice and cook the chicken to 165F; rest and slice.
  2. Stir yogurt, salsa, and lime into a dressing.
  3. Bowl the greens and tomatoes; add chicken, avocado, and cheddar.
  4. Serve with dressing and extra salsa; keep dressing separate if prepping ahead.

Note: Avocado and cheddar portions drive the calories — divide them evenly.
