Korean-Style Beef and Cabbage

Makes 2 servings. About 650-750 kcal, 55-65 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
93% lean ground beef 1½ lb (680 g)
thinly sliced green cabbage 6 cups
baby spinach 4 packed cups
eggs 2 large
scallions, sliced 3
garlic cloves, minced 3
grated fresh ginger 1 tbsp
low-sodium soy sauce 2 tbsp
sesame oil 2 tsp
rice vinegar 1 tbsp
mild gochujang, optional 1–2 tsp
Black pepper

Process

  1. Brown the ground beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it into small pieces. Drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Add the garlic and ginger; cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in the soy sauce, rice vinegar, sesame oil, and optional gochujang.
  4. Add the cabbage and cook for 4–6 minutes, until tender but still slightly crisp. Fold in the spinach and allow it to wilt.
  5. In a separate nonstick skillet, scramble the eggs. Fold them into the beef mixture or serve them on top.
  6. Divide into two portions and finish with scallions.

Note: Start with the smallest amount of gochujang. It adds flavor, but the heat level varies by brand.
