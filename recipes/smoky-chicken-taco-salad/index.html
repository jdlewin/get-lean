Smoky Chicken Taco Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 65-75 g protein, 20-30 g carbs, 22-30 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 4 cups chopped romaine + 2 cups baby spinach
  • 1 cup chopped tomatoes
  • 1 medium avocado
  • 40 g shredded cheddar
  • 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt
  • 1/4 cup salsa, plus extra to serve
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + salt and pepper

Process

  1. Rub the chicken with cumin, smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Sear or grill to 165F; rest and slice.
  2. Stir yogurt, salsa, and lime into a dressing; thin with water if needed.
  3. Bowl the greens and tomatoes; add chicken, diced avocado, and cheddar, dividing the avocado and cheddar evenly.
  4. Serve with dressing and extra salsa; keep dressing separate if prepping ahead.

Note: Avocado and cheddar portions drive the calories — measure and divide them evenly.
