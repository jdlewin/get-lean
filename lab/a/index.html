lab / week 2 sunday cook (~90 min) — experiment, not the live cookbook

Layout A — Timeline

One cook, one clock. Stage 0 happens before the clock — about 20 min, no heat, just knife work and jars. Then T+0: the oven goes on and everything else fills the gaps. Quantities are the full batch as written.

WhenDoDetail
Stage 0Wash + dry.All greens, cucumbers, tomatoes, zucchini, celery. Box the greens with a paper towel. Keep the raw-meat board separate from here on.
Stage 0Cut the veg.Cauliflower into florets. Cabbage sliced, about 6 cups, boxed dry. Cucumbers diced. Cherry tomatoes halved. Zucchini in half-moons. Celery sliced.
Stage 0Aromatics.Garlic minced, ginger grated — little piles per recipe (chicken, meatballs, dressings, later-week beef).
Stage 0Spices measured.Oregano + Italian seasoning for the chicken and meatballs today; shawarma spices (cumin, paprika, coriander, turmeric) into small containers for later in the week.
Stage 0Containers + labels.Monday/Tuesday boxes out. Freezer portions labeled with recipe name and date. Three small jars out for dressings.
T+0:00Oven to 425°F. Cauliflower in.1 head in florets, half the oil, salt, pepper. Roast 25–35 min, turn once. (Curry or steak dinner — keep portions separate once cooked.)
T+0:05Salad chicken on.1.5 lb chicken, 2 tsp oregano, half the garlic, salt, pepper. Skillet over medium-high to 165°F. Rest 5 min, slice. This covers Mon + Tue lunches.
T+0:15Meatballs mixed and in.1.5 lb turkey, 1 egg, 30 g parm, half the garlic, 2 tsp Italian seasoning, salt, pepper. Roll ~12, parchment tray, same 425°F oven, 15–20 min to 165°F.
T+0:20Tomato sauce simmering.1 tbsp oil, remaining garlic + 1 tsp oregano 30 sec. 28 oz crushed tomatoes, salt, pepper. Simmer 15 min.
T+0:25Three dressings into jars.Greek lemon: 2 tbsp oil + juice of 1 lemon + 1 tsp Dijon + remaining garlic + pepper. Ginger-lime peanut: 2 tbsp PB + 2 tbsp soy + juice of 2 limes + 1 tbsp vinegar + 2 tsp sesame oil + garlic + ginger, thin with water. Shawarma yogurt: 1 cup yogurt + half-lemon juice + salt + pepper.
T+0:30Cabbage sliced and boxed.About 6 cups, dry, sealed container. Feeds the Wed slaw and Fri Korean beef.
T+0:35Cauliflower out. Meatballs out.Meatballs into the sauce for 5 min. Cauliflower into shallow containers so it cools fast.
T+0:45Zucchini seared. Portion everything.2 zucchini in half-moons, browned in a skillet. Chicken-salad greens boxed Mon/Tue (dressing separate). Meatballs + sauce split in two — dinner for two tonight.

Leave for later

  • Shrimp and salmon stay frozen. Thaw each day's portion overnight in the fridge; cook shrimp 1–2 min per side just before eating.
  • Burger potatoes and asparagus roast fresh later in the week.
  • Stuffed peppers, garlic-lemon shrimp, shawarma, and burger bowls cook on their days — seasonings pre-measured in small containers if you like.
