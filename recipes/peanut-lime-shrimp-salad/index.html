Peanut-Lime Shrimp Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 500-600 kcal, 60-70 g protein, 20-30 g carbs, 15-22 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb peeled, deveined shrimp
  • 4 cups thinly sliced cabbage
  • 1 cucumber
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 2 tbsp natural peanut butter
  • 2 tsp sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • Juice of 2 limes
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 minced garlic cloves + 1 tsp grated ginger
  • Optional scallions + sesame seeds

Process

  1. Thaw shrimp, pat dry. Whisk peanut butter, soy, lime, vinegar, garlic, and ginger; thin with water a teaspoon at a time until pourable.
  2. Heat sesame oil; sear shrimp 1-2 minutes per side until opaque.
  3. Cut cucumber into matchsticks. Toss cabbage, cucumber, and spinach with most of the dressing; top with shrimp.
  4. Finish with remaining dressing and scallions; refrigerate components separately if prepping ahead.

Note: Peanut butter is calorie-dense — measure it, don't free-pour. Mild, tangy sauce, not spicy.
