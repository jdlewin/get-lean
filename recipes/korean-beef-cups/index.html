Korean Beef Lettuce Cups

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 70-85 g protein, 20-35 g carbs, 40-55 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Ground beef, 93% lean 1.5 lb
Eggs 4 large
Butter lettuce or romaine 1 large head
Broccoli florets 1 lb
Cucumber 1
Soy sauce, low-sodium 2 tbsp
Sesame oil 1 tsp
Garlic cloves, minced 3
Fresh ginger, grated 1 tbsp
Rice vinegar 1 tbsp
Scallions, sliced 2
Kimchi, mild (optional) 2-4 tbsp

Process

  1. Steam or roast the broccoli until tender-crisp. Cook the eggs to preference — jammy means simmering about 7 minutes, then an ice-water bath — and halve.
  2. Brown the beef in a skillet, breaking it up; drain excess fat. Cook the garlic and ginger 30 seconds in the beef.
  3. Stir in the soy sauce, vinegar, and measured sesame oil, with a splash of water if dry; taste before adding more.
  4. Separate the lettuce leaves and slice the cucumber. Serve the beef in the leaves with cucumber, broccoli, and halved eggs; top with scallions, kimchi on the side if using.

Note: Kimchi adds tang and sodium, not protein — keep it optional and mild.
