Peanut-Lime Shrimp Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 500-600 kcal, 60-70 g protein, 20-30 g carbs, 15-22 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Shrimp, peeled and deveined 1.5 lb
Cabbage, thinly sliced 4 cups
Cucumber 1
Baby spinach 4 cups
Peanut butter, natural 2 tbsp
Sesame oil 2 tsp
Soy sauce, low-sodium 2 tbsp
Lime juice Juice of 2 limes
Rice vinegar 1 tbsp
Garlic cloves, minced 2
Fresh ginger, grated 1 tsp
Scallions (optional) to taste
Sesame seeds (optional) to taste

Process

  1. Whisk the measured peanut butter with soy, lime, vinegar, garlic, and ginger; thin with water a teaspoon at a time until pourable.
  2. Thaw the shrimp and pat dry; sear in hot sesame oil 1-2 minutes per side until opaque.
  3. Cut the cucumber into matchsticks. Toss the cabbage, cucumber, and spinach with most of the dressing; top with the shrimp.
  4. Finish with the remaining dressing, sliced scallions, and a sprinkle of sesame seeds. Refrigerate components separately if prepping ahead.

Note: Peanut butter is calorie-dense — measure it, don't free-pour. Mild, tangy sauce, not spicy.
