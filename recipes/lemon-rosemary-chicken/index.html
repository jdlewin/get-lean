Lemon-Rosemary Chicken & Spinach

Makes 2 servings. About 600-750 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breastSeason with rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper; sear to 165F and rest.
1 medium cauliflowerCut into florets, toss with half the oil and rosemary, and roast at 425F for 25-35 minutes.
10 oz baby spinachWilt in a separate pan with garlic, adding by handfuls.
1 tbsp olive oil, dividedHalf for the cauliflower roast, half for the spinach pan.
1 tbsp butterMelt in the chicken skillet for the sauce.
4 minced garlic cloves, dividedHalf in the pan sauce (30 seconds), half in the spinach pan.
1 lemon, juiced and zestedSimmer juice and zest into the pan sauce.
1/2 cup low-sodium chicken stockAdd to the skillet and simmer 2-3 minutes, scraping up browned bits.
1 tsp dried (or 1 tbsp fresh) rosemary + thyme, salt, pepperSeason the chicken and cauliflower.

Process

  1. Roast cauliflower at 425F, 25-35 minutes.
  2. Sear seasoned chicken to 165F; rest on a plate.
  3. Cook half the garlic 30 seconds in butter; add stock, lemon juice, and zest; simmer 2-3 minutes.
  4. Wilt spinach with remaining oil and garlic; serve chicken with pan sauce, spinach, and cauliflower.

Note: Spoon the lemon sauce over the chicken, not the vegetables, to keep portions consistent.
