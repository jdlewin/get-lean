Mild Red-Curry Salmon and Shrimp with Spinach

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
salmon fillet, cut into two portions 1 lb (454 g)
peeled, deveined shrimp 12 oz (340 g)
baby spinach 10 oz (280 g)
cauliflower, cut into florets 1 medium
light coconut milk ½ cup
Maesri red curry paste, starting with less if heat-sensitive 2 tsp
garlic cloves, minced 2
olive oil 1 tsp
Juice of 1 lime
low-sodium chicken broth or water ½ cup
fish sauce or low-sodium soy sauce, optional 1 tsp
Salt and black pepper

Shopping list

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Toss the cauliflower with half the olive oil, salt, and pepper. Roast for 25–35 minutes, turning once.
  2. Season the salmon with black pepper. Bake on a lined tray for approximately 10–15 minutes, depending on thickness, until it reaches 145°F / 63°C or your preferred safe doneness.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in a large skillet. Add the garlic and curry paste and stir for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the coconut milk and broth. Stir until smooth and simmer gently for 3–4 minutes.
  5. Add the shrimp and cook until opaque and cooked through. Fold in the spinach until wilted.
  6. Add the lime juice and optional fish sauce or soy sauce. Serve half the curry mixture with one salmon portion and half the roasted cauliflower.

Note: Curry pastes vary considerably in heat and sodium. Start with 1 tsp if needed, then taste before adding more.
