About 2 hours, most of it hands-off. Gets Mon–Wed packed in the fridge and the rest in the freezer. Quantities below are the full 2-serving batch as written in each linked recipe.

1. Set up (15 min)

Clear the counters and heat the oven to 425°F. Wash all the produce. Keep raw meat away from the salad greens — separate boards.

2. Roast veg (35–45 min, mostly hands-off)

Cauliflower and Brussels for Mon–Tue, Garlic-Parmesan Chicken & Veg veg method: cut the cauliflower into florets and halve the Brussels sprouts; toss with olive oil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Roast at 425°F for 25–35 minutes, turning once, until browned at the edges. Give them room — two trays if needed. Wash and dry the spinach, arugula, and romaine while the oven runs; store greens with a paper towel in sealed boxes.

3. Cook the lunch proteins (30–45 min)

Gochujang Beef, Cabbage & Spinach (Monday lunch, full batch): brown 1.5 lb beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up; drain excess fat if needed. Mince 3 garlic cloves and grate 1 tbsp ginger; cook 30 seconds in the beef fat. Stir in 2 tbsp soy sauce, 2 tsp measured gochujang, and 1 tbsp rice vinegar; season with pepper. Add 6 cups sliced cabbage and cook 4–6 minutes until softened but still a little crisp; fold in 4 packed cups spinach last, just until wilted. Cool fast and fridge it. Fry the 2 eggs fresh at serving, not this morning.

Mediterranean chicken (Mediterranean Chicken Salad): season 1.5 lb chicken with oregano, salt, and pepper; grill or sear to 165°F. Rest, then slice. Season on its own so it tastes different from the taco batch.

Smoky taco chicken (Smoky Chicken Taco Salad): coat 1.5 lb chicken with 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp garlic powder, salt, and pepper; sear or grill to 165°F. Rest and slice.

Dressings into small jars. Peanut-lime (Peanut-Lime Shrimp Salad): whisk 2 tbsp measured peanut butter with 2 tbsp soy, juice of 2 limes, 1 tbsp vinegar, 2 minced garlic cloves, and 1 tsp grated ginger; thin with water a teaspoon at a time until pourable. Taco: stir 1/2 cup yogurt with 1/4 cup salsa and a squeeze of lime; thin with water if needed.

Shrimp note: save the shrimp for closer to the day — sear thawed, patted-dry shrimp in hot sesame oil 1–2 minutes per side until opaque — or freeze it right after it cools.

4. Portion and pack (15 min)

Split every recipe in two equal portions. Dressings, wet toppings, and greens stay separate till serving. Fridge what you'll eat in 3–4 days; freeze the rest. Fish stays frozen till the night before, then thaws in the fridge.

5. Wednesday refresh (15–25 min)

Fresh greens and cucumber. Cook the shrimp lunches. Move Fri–Sun boxes from freezer to fridge. Roast more veg if the first batch is gone.

Keep heat mild; no mushrooms, green beans, cauliflower mash/rice. Measure hummus, peanut butter, nuts, oils, cheese.

Batch prep

Sunday cook, one page: 425°F oven; roast cauliflower + halved Brussels 25–35 min; gochujang beef skillet (cabbage 4–6 min, spinach wilted last, eggs fried fresh at serving); Mediterranean chicken (oregano/salt/pepper, 165°F) and smoky taco chicken (cumin/paprika/garlic powder, 165°F) cooked and seasoned separately; peanut-lime and taco dressings bottled. Portion everything in two, dressings and greens separate. Refrigerate what you'll eat in 3–4 days, freeze the rest. Wednesday: refresh greens, cook the shrimp lunch, thaw Fri–Sun portions.
