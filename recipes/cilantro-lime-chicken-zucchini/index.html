Cilantro-Lime Chicken with Roasted Zucchini

Makes 2 servings. About 500-550 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
chicken breast 1½ lb
zucchini 4 medium
olive oil 1 tbsp
juice and zest of 2 limes
garlic cloves 3
cumin 1 tsp
smoked paprika 1 tsp
cilantro ¼ cup
salt and pepper

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss zucchini with about half the oil and roast 20–25 minutes.
  2. Season chicken with lime, garlic, cumin, paprika, cilantro, salt and pepper. Sear in remaining oil and finish cooking to 165°F internally.
  3. Rest 5 minutes and divide chicken and zucchini evenly.
