Tex-Mex Chicken Skillet

Makes 2 servings. About 650-800 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breastDice, season with the spices, and sear in batches to 165F.
2 bell peppersSlice and cook 5-7 minutes with the onion until softened.
1 medium yellow onionSlice and cook with the peppers.
1 medium avocadoDice and divide evenly between servings; measure.
40 g shredded cheddarDivide evenly; measure.
4 cups shredded romaineServe the skillet over it as the base.
1/2 cup salsa (mild)Stir into the skillet with lime and simmer 1-2 minutes.
1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurtThin with lime juice and water into a crema for topping.
1 tsp olive oilHeat for searing the chicken; measure.
2 tsp cumin + 1 tsp smoked paprika + 1 tsp garlic powder + lime juiceSeason the chicken; finish the skillet with salsa and lime.

Process

  1. Season diced chicken with the spices.
  2. Sear in a single layer (batches if needed) to 165F.
  3. Add peppers and onion 5-7 minutes; stir in salsa and lime, simmer 1-2 minutes.
  4. Serve over romaine; top with avocado, cheddar, and yogurt crema.

Note: Keep the salsa mild; measure avocado and cheese and split evenly.
