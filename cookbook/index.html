The Kitchen

Sunday prep, Week 1 shopping checklist, and recipe index. Part of The Plan. Every recipe makes 2 servings — one cook covers two days. Full plan text: november-wk1-cut-2026-10-28.

Sunday prep

Set up and preheat to 425°F; roast cauliflower + Brussels for Mon–Tue; cook the gochujang beef and the salad chickens (season separately); bottle the peanut-lime and taco dressings; portion everything in two, dressings and greens separate. Refrigerate what you'll eat in 3–4 days, freeze the rest. Wednesday: refresh greens, cook the shrimp lunch, thaw Fri–Sun portions. Full schedule in the frozen source.

Holidays

Holiday weeks aren't for losing — they're for not starting January in a hole. The cap is ~1 lb over baseline (the protocol's 0.5 kg ceiling, rounded). The reason is the stickiness: holiday gains are modest, but people never take them back off, and that's what piles up year after year.

  • Eat the dinner. Skip the week-long leftovers binge.
  • Keep weighing every morning, holidays included — catch it the week it happens, not in February.
  • If the average ticks up, back on template until it's gone. A pound is a few days, not a ruined month.

Keep heat mild; no mushrooms, green beans, cauliflower mash/rice. Measure hummus, peanut butter, nuts, oils, cheese.
