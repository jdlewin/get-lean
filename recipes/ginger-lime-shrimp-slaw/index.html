Ginger-Lime Shrimp Slaw

Makes 2 servings. About 450-550 kcal, 55-65 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
peeled, deveined shrimp 1½ lb (680 g)
green cabbage, thinly sliced 1 small
use about 6 packed cups
baby spinach 4 packed cups
cucumber, cut into matchsticks 1
natural peanut butter 2 tbsp
low-sodium soy sauce 2 tbsp
Juice of 2 limes
rice vinegar 1 tbsp
sesame oil 2 tsp
garlic cloves, minced 2
grated fresh ginger 1 tbsp
olive oil 1 tsp
Black pepper

Process

  1. Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, and ginger. Add 1–3 tbsp warm water until pourable.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet. Pat the shrimp dry, season with black pepper, and cook in a single layer for approximately 1–2 minutes per side, until opaque and cooked through.
  3. Combine the cabbage, spinach, and cucumber in a large bowl.
  4. Divide the slaw and shrimp between two portions. Add the dressing just before eating.

Note: For the best texture, store the cooked shrimp separately from the vegetables and dressing. If you want more crunch, reserve some cabbage to add fresh on serving day.
