Gochujang Beef, Cabbage & Spinach

Makes 2 servings. About 650-750 kcal, 60-70 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

1.5 lb 93% lean ground beefBrown in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, and drain excess fat.
6 cups thinly sliced green cabbageAdd after the aromatics and cook 4-6 minutes until softened but still a little crisp.
4 packed cups baby spinachFold in last and cook just until wilted.
2 large eggsCook to your preference in a separate nonstick pan.
3 scallionsSlice thin for topping.
3 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp grated gingerMince the garlic and grate the ginger, then cook 30 seconds in the beef fat.
2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauceStir in with the gochujang and vinegar to season the beef.
2 tsp gochujang to startMeasure (don't free-pour); add more only if desired.
2 tsp sesame oilDrizzle a little over each finished portion.
1 tbsp rice vinegar + black pepperStir the vinegar into the beef; season with pepper.

Process

  1. Brown the beef, breaking it up; drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, then stir in soy sauce, gochujang, and rice vinegar.
  3. Add cabbage 4-6 minutes until crisp-tender; fold in spinach until wilted.
  4. Divide into two portions, top each with one egg, scallions, and sesame oil.

Note: Calories hinge on the beef's fat content and whether fat is drained. Keep gochujang mild and measured.
