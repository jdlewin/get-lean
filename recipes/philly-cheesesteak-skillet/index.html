Philly Cheesesteak Skillet (dinner)

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of the plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb lean sirloin — Chill briefly, slice thin across the grain, pat dry, and season with pepper and garlic powder.
  • 2 bell peppers — Slice and cook 6-8 minutes until softened and lightly browned.
  • 1 medium yellow onion — Slice and cook with the peppers.
  • 4 slices provolone — Lay two over each portion at the end and cover briefly to melt.
  • 2 tsp olive oil, divided — Use 1 tsp for searing steak, the rest for the vegetables; measure.
  • 2 cups broccoli florets — Steam or microwave until tender-crisp; serve alongside.
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire + 1 tsp garlic powder + black pepper — Toss the returned steak in the Worcestershire; season with the rest.

Process

  1. Slice steak thin (chilling first helps); pat dry and season.
  2. Sear steak in batches so it browns; set aside on a plate.
  3. Cook peppers and onion 6-8 minutes; cook broccoli separately.
  4. Return steak, toss with Worcestershire, portion in two, top each with two provolone slices, cover to melt, and serve with broccoli.

Note: Use lean trimmed sirloin and measured oil. No bread needed — peppers and onions give the cheesesteak flavor.
