Greek Chicken Meatball Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 500-600 kcal, 70-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
98–99% lean ground chicken 1½ lb
egg 1
diced onion ¼ cup
garlic cloves 2
oregano 2 tsp
zest of 1 lemon
olive oil 1 tbsp
arugula/mixed greens 5 oz
cucumber 1
tomatoes 2
nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
lemon juice 1 tbsp
dill 1 tsp
salt and pepper

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Mix chicken, egg, onion, garlic, oregano, lemon zest, salt and pepper; form 12 meatballs.
  2. Lightly coat with measured oil and bake 15–20 minutes, to 165°F internally.
  3. Mix yogurt, lemon juice and dill. Divide greens, cucumber and tomato between two containers; add 6 meatballs per serving and keep sauce separate.
