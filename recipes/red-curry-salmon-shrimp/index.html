Red Curry Salmon & Shrimp (dinner)

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 70-85 g protein per serving. Part of the plan.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb salmon fillet — Cut into 2 portions, season lightly, and bake about 10-14 minutes until just cooked through.
  • 12 oz peeled, deveined shrimp — Simmer in the curry 3-5 minutes until opaque.
  • 1 medium cauliflower — Cut into florets, toss with oil, salt, and pepper; roast at 425F for 25-35 minutes.
  • 10 oz baby spinach — Fold into the curry at the end until wilted.
  • 1/2 cup light coconut milk — Simmer with the curry paste 2-3 minutes as the sauce.
  • 2 tsp Maesri red curry paste to start — Gently cook with garlic before adding coconut milk; add more only if wanted hotter.
  • 1 tsp neutral oil — Toss with the cauliflower for roasting.
  • 2 minced garlic cloves — Cook 30 seconds before the curry paste goes in.
  • Juice of 1 lime + 1 tsp fish sauce (or soy) + water — Stir in to finish; splash water if the sauce is thick.

Process

  1. Roast cauliflower at 425F, 25-35 minutes until browned and tender.
  2. Bake salmon on a lined tray, 10-14 minutes depending on thickness.
  3. Cook garlic 30 seconds; stir in curry paste, coconut milk, and water; simmer 2-3 minutes.
  4. Add shrimp 3-5 minutes until opaque; stir in fish sauce and lime; fold in spinach.
  5. Plate one salmon portion per person with half the shrimp curry, spinach, and cauliflower.

Note: Paste strength varies — start with 2 tsp for the whole recipe and taste first.
