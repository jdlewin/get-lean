Red Curry Salmon & Shrimp

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 70-85 g protein, 20-35 g carbs, 30-45 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Salmon fillet 1 lb
Shrimp, peeled and deveined 12 oz
Cauliflower 1 medium
Baby spinach 10 oz
Coconut milk, light 1/2 cup
Red curry paste (Maesri) 2 tsp, to start
Neutral oil 1 tsp
Garlic cloves, minced 2
Lime juice Juice of 1 lime
Fish sauce (or soy) 1 tsp
Water as needed

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425F. Cut the cauliflower into florets; toss with the oil, salt, and pepper and roast 25-35 minutes until browned and tender.
  2. Cut the salmon into 2 portions and season lightly; bake on a lined tray 10-14 minutes depending on thickness, until just cooked through.
  3. Cook the garlic 30 seconds; gently stir in the curry paste before adding the coconut milk and water. Simmer 2-3 minutes as the sauce.
  4. Add the shrimp for 3-5 minutes until opaque; stir in the fish sauce and lime, then fold in the spinach until wilted.
  5. Plate one salmon portion per person with half the shrimp curry, spinach, and cauliflower.

Note: Paste strength varies — start with 2 tsp for the whole recipe and taste before adding more.
