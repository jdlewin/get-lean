Turkey Meatballs with Tomato Sauce and Zucchini

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 55-70 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
93% lean ground turkey 1½ lb (680 g)
egg 1 large
finely grated Parmesan 30 g
garlic cloves, minced 3
Italian seasoning 2 tsp
crushed tomatoes 1 can (28 oz / 794 g)
zucchini, cut into thick half-moons 2 medium
olive oil 1 tbsp
dried oregano 1 tsp
Salt and black pepper
Optional: ¼ tsp red pepper flakes; omit if avoiding heat

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Line a baking sheet with parchment.
  2. Combine the turkey, egg, Parmesan, half the garlic, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Mix gently and form approximately 12 meatballs.
  3. Bake for 15–20 minutes, or until the center of the meatballs reaches 165°F / 74°C.
  4. While the meatballs bake, heat the olive oil in a saucepan. Add the remaining garlic and oregano and cook for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the crushed tomatoes, season with salt and pepper, and simmer for 15 minutes.
  6. Add the cooked meatballs to the sauce and simmer for another 5 minutes.
  7. In a separate skillet, cook the zucchini until lightly browned and tender. Divide the meatballs, sauce, and zucchini evenly between two servings.

Note: No breadcrumbs are needed. Keep the meatball mixture loose enough to avoid dense meatballs, but do not overmix.
