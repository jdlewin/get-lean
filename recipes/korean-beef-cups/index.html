Korean Beef Lettuce Cups (dinner)

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 70-85 g protein per serving. Part of the plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb 93% lean ground beef — Brown in a skillet, breaking it up, and drain excess fat.
  • 4 large eggs — Cook to preference (jammy: simmer about 7 minutes, then ice-water bath) and halve.
  • 1 large head butter lettuce or romaine — Separate leaves for cups.
  • 1 lb broccoli florets — Steam or roast until tender-crisp.
  • 1 cucumber — Slice for the cups.
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce — Stir into the beef with vinegar and sesame oil; taste before adding more.
  • 1 tsp sesame oil — Stir into the beef; measure.
  • 3 minced garlic cloves + 1 tbsp grated ginger — Cook 30 seconds in the beef after browning.
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 sliced scallions — Stir vinegar into the beef; top with scallions.
  • Optional mild kimchi, 2-4 tbsp — Serve on the side.

Process

  1. Cook broccoli; cook eggs to preference and halve.
  2. Brown beef and drain; add garlic and ginger 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in soy, vinegar, and sesame oil (splash of water if dry); taste first.
  4. Serve beef in lettuce leaves with cucumber, broccoli, and halved eggs; top with scallions and optional kimchi.

Note: Kimchi adds tang and sodium, not protein — keep it optional and mild.
