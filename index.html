The Plan

A concerted effort for G-Force and JD to cease being sad sacks and become chrizzled beasts.

1. Competition

Seventeen weeks. November to March. First DEXA scan was on 05NOV. Scan again in March. Whoever loses the greatest percentage of body fat between the two scans is the winner.

Same machine, same operator, both times. Same prep both times: fasted 8+ hours (water only), normal water intake, no hard workout the day before, same morning time.

Weekly Discipline

  • Weigh in every morning. Same scale, same clothes, ideally after blasting a shit. Judge the 7-day average, not any single day.
  • Hit your protein number daily. Write down your training each week. Sleep 7+ hours.
  • Holiday weeks aren't for losing — eat the dinner, skip the week-long leftovers binge, and get back on template until any gain is gone. See the kitchen for more.

Loss rate

  • Aim about 1 to 1.5 lb a week.
  • Faster than ~1% of bodyweight loss per two weeks risks slowing down the plan.

Call the clinician

Chest pain or pressure. Fainting. Blood pressure 180/120 or higher. Steady dizziness or deep fatigue. Sleep under 5 hours three nights running

2. Diet

The thinking

Protein is the lever. Past ~1.1 g per lb of bodyweight a day adds nothing. Calorie calculators run 10%+ off the person — weigh daily and steer by the trend, not the app.

Daily protein

25 y/o 0.7–0.8 g per lb · 35 y/o 0.8–1.0 g per lb · 45 y/o 1.0–1.1 g per lb. Work it off goal weight (or fat-free mass from the DEXA), not current weight — protein feeds muscle, not fat. That puts the 45 y/o near 185–245 g a day across 4 meals, roughly 25–60 g under the old current-weight figure. Recheck the target every 10–20 lb lost.

The template

November Week 1 cut: two cooked meals a day + one whey shake. Planning target ~2,000 cal a day, not a hard ceiling — steer by morning weight, hunger, and lifting. - 12:00 PM — lunch, cooked meal - 3:00–4:00 PM — whey isolate shake (~30–35 g protein) - 5:30–6:30 PM — dinner, cooked meal - No bread, pasta, or rice (easy to overeat); carbs from veg, yogurt, sauces, optional fruit.

The dishes

Lunches — Gochujang beef & cabbage, Mediterranean chicken salad, Smoky taco salad, Peanut-lime shrimp salad; dinners — Philly cheesesteak skillet, Garlic-Parmesan chicken, Red curry salmon & shrimp, Korean beef lettuce cups, Steakhouse sirloin, Tex-Mex chicken skillet, Lemon-rosemary chicken. See the cookbook for more.

Shake + snack

One whey isolate shake daily. Optional snack: blueberries, boiled egg, cucumber + measured hummus, or plain nonfat Greek yogurt.

Watch-outs

Per-serving estimates run 500–900 kcal — two meals + shake + snack can run past 2,000, so judge by the scale. Most days may land short of protein — weigh portions, confirm shake label, add lean protein as needed.

3. Exercise

The thinking

Activity starts light while getting used to eating less. As sleep, strength, and mood hold up load increases. Lifting while eating less keeps muscle: 10+ sets per muscle a week, 2+ sessions, full range of motion. Keep lifting and conditioning 3+ hours apart, and let daily steps do the calorie work, not extra cardio. Sessions are per week: weeks 1–4 means 2 lifting days and 3 cardio days out of 7.

Every couple of months a planned easy week — same moves, half the sets, no added load — drains built-up fatigue so the next block climbs higher. Skipping it stalls you; taking it makes you stronger the gym.

Conditioning

Two gears, both simple. Easy cardio is a 35–45 minute brisk walk (or bike, or a walk with a weighted pack) — fast enough to warm you, slow enough to talk. Recovery is a 20-minute easy walk plus 10 minutes of stretching. All of this counts toward the daily step total — the 8,000 (or 10,000, or 12,000) is the whole day, not steps-plus-walk.

Lifting

Seven moves do nearly all the work: squat, Bulgarian split squat, push-up, row, single-leg RDL, plank + side plank, farmer carry. Fewer moves means each one gets loaded heavier over time, and loaded muscle is kept muscle. Anything else is garnish.

Taper

Hard training leaves muscles inflamed and holding water, which can muddy the final scan. The last week goes easy so the March reading shows real tissue, not swelling. You test your maxes fresh instead of fried.
