Mediterranean Chicken Salad (lunch)

Makes 2 servings. About 550-650 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of operation-get-lean.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast — Season with oregano, salt, and pepper; grill or sear to 165F, rest, then slice.
  • 4 cups arugula + 4 cups baby spinach — Divide between two containers as the salad base.
  • 1 medium cucumber — Chop and split between the two containers.
  • 1 small apple, diced (optional) — Add just before eating so it stays fresh; omit for fewer carbs.
  • 20 g pecans — Chop and divide between servings.
  • 1 tbsp olive oil — Whisk into the dressing.
  • 2 tsp Dijon mustard — Whisk into the dressing for bite.
  • Juice of 1 lemon (or 2 tbsp red-wine vinegar) — Whisk into the dressing; add water to thin.
  • 1 garlic clove + oregano, salt, pepper — Grate the garlic fine into the dressing; season the chicken with the rest.

Process

  1. Season chicken and cook to 165F; rest, then slice.
  2. Whisk oil, Dijon, lemon, garlic, and pepper (thin with water if needed).
  3. Box the greens, cucumber, chicken, and pecans; keep dressing separate.
  4. Add apple just before eating.

Note: Omit the apple to keep carbs lower — lemon, Dijon, and pecans carry the flavor.
