Garlic Shrimp with Lemon Spinach and Potatoes

Makes 2 servings. About 550-650 kcal, 70-85 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
peeled, deveined shrimp 1½ lb
baby potatoes, halved 12 oz
spinach 10 oz
olive oil 1 tbsp
butter 1 tbsp
garlic cloves 4
Juice and zest of 1 lemon; ½ tsp smoked paprika; salt and pepper

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss potatoes with half the oil and roast 25–35 minutes.
  2. Season shrimp with paprika, salt and pepper. Cook in remaining oil in batches, 1–2 minutes per side, until opaque. Remove.
  3. Add butter and garlic to pan; cook 30 seconds. Wilt spinach in handfuls, add lemon, return shrimp and toss. Divide all components evenly.
