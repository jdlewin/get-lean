Smoked Salmon, Eggs and Yogurt Plate

Makes 1 serving. About 500-600 kcal, 55-65 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
nonfat Greek yogurt 1½ cups
smoked salmon 4 oz
eggs 2
cucumber 1 cup
tomato 1 medium
arugula 1 cup
olive oil 1 tsp
lemon juice
dill
black pepper

Process

  1. Boil eggs to preferred doneness, cool and peel.
  2. Season yogurt with lemon, dill and pepper. Serve with salmon, eggs, cucumber, tomato and arugula dressed with the measured oil.
  3. Smoked salmon is salty; taste before adding salt.
