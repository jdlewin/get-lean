Garlic-Lemon Shrimp with Broccoli

Makes 2 servings. About 450-550 kcal, 55-65 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
peeled, deveined shrimp 1½ lb (680 g)
broccoli florets 1½ lb (680 g)
olive oil, divided 2 tbsp
garlic cloves, minced 4
Juice and zest of 1 lemon
plain nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
Dijon mustard 1 tsp
dried oregano 1 tsp
Salt and black pepper

Shopping list

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Toss the broccoli with 1 tbsp olive oil, half the garlic, salt, and pepper.
  2. Roast for 20–25 minutes, turning once, until browned at the edges.
  3. Mix the Greek yogurt, Dijon, lemon zest, half the lemon juice, oregano, and black pepper. Add a teaspoon or two of water if needed to thin it.
  4. Pat the shrimp dry. Heat the remaining olive oil in a large skillet and cook the remaining garlic for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the shrimp and cook for approximately 1–2 minutes per side, until opaque and cooked through. Finish with the remaining lemon juice.
  6. Divide the shrimp and broccoli between two plates and serve with half the yogurt sauce per serving.

Note: Keep the yogurt sauce cold and separate until serving. Avoid cooking it directly in the hot pan, as it may split.
