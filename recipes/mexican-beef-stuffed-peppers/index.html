Mild Mexican Beef-Stuffed Peppers

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 55-65 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
93–96% lean ground beef 1½ lb
bell peppers, halved and seeded 3
canned diced tomatoes 1 cup
onion ½ small
garlic cloves 2
cumin 2 tsp
smoked paprika 2 tsp
oregano ½ tsp
reduced-fat cheddar 2 oz
olive oil 1 tsp
salt and pepper

Process

  1. Heat oven to 400°F. Bake pepper halves cut-side up for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat oil; brown beef. Add onion, garlic, tomatoes and spices; cook until beef reaches 160°F and liquid reduces.
  3. Fill pepper halves, divide cheddar over tops and bake 15–20 minutes. Serve three halves per portion.
