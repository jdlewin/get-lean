Beef-Stuffed Bell Peppers

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 55-70 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
93% lean ground beef 1½ lb (680 g)
bell peppers, halved lengthwise and seeded 4 large
diced tomatoes, drained slightly 1 can (14.5 oz / 411 g)
baby spinach, chopped 4 packed cups
part-skim shredded mozzarella 100 g
garlic cloves, minced 3
Italian seasoning 2 tsp
olive oil 1 tbsp
Salt and black pepper

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F / 205°C. Arrange the pepper halves cut-side up in a baking dish.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a skillet. Brown the beef, breaking it up as it cooks. Add garlic and Italian seasoning and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in the diced tomatoes and spinach. Cook until the spinach wilts and excess liquid reduces. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Spoon the mixture evenly into the pepper halves.
  5. Cover loosely with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover, top with mozzarella, and bake another 10–15 minutes, until the peppers are tender and the beef mixture is hot.
  6. Divide the four pepper halves evenly between two servings.

Note: This recipe uses no rice or breadcrumbs. Drain excess liquid from the tomatoes so the filling does not become watery.
