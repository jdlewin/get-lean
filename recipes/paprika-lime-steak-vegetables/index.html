Paprika-Lime Steak with Roasted Vegetables

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 60-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
lean trimmed sirloin 1½ lb
zucchini 2 medium
bell peppers 2
red onion 1
olive oil 1 tbsp
smoked paprika 2 tsp
cumin 1 tsp
Juice of 1 lime; 2 garlic cloves; salt and pepper

Shopping list

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss vegetables with about half the oil, paprika, salt and pepper; roast 20–25 minutes.
  2. Season steak with cumin, garlic, lime, salt and pepper. Sear in a hot skillet with remaining oil to preferred doneness, using a thermometer.
  3. Rest 5–10 minutes, slice against the grain and divide steak and vegetables evenly.
