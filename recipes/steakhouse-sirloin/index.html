Steakhouse Sirloin & Veg

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 65-80 g protein, 20-35 g carbs, 40-55 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Sirloin steaks, trimmed 1.5 lb
Asparagus 1 lb
Cauliflower 1 medium
Arugula 4 cups
Olive oil, divided 1 tbsp
Butter 1 tbsp
Blue cheese (optional) 1 oz total
Lemon 1
Dijon mustard 1 tsp
Garlic clove 1
Salt and pepper to taste

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425F. Cut the cauliflower into florets; toss with half the oil and roast 25-35 minutes.
  2. Season the steaks with salt and pepper; sear or grill to preference. Rest at least 5-10 minutes, then slice.
  3. Trim the woody ends from the asparagus; roast or pan-sear until tender-crisp. Toss the arugula with lemon, Dijon, garlic, and the remaining oil.
  4. Finish the rested steaks with a small amount of butter. Plate with the vegetables, splitting the blue cheese evenly if using.

Note: Cut, trimming, and butter swing the calories — skip the blue cheese for a lighter plate.
