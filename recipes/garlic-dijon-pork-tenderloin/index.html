Garlic-Dijon Pork Tenderloin with Brussels Sprouts

Makes 2 servings. About 475-550 kcal, 60-70 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
trimmed pork tenderloin 1½ lb
Brussels sprouts, halved 1 lb
olive oil 1 tbsp
Dijon 1 tbsp
garlic cloves 3
dried thyme 1 tsp
apple cider vinegar 1 tbsp
salt and pepper

Shopping list

Process

  1. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss sprouts with half the oil and roast 20–25 minutes.
  2. Mix Dijon, garlic, thyme and vinegar; coat pork. Sear using remaining oil, then roast to 145°F. Rest at least 3 minutes.
  3. Slice and divide pork and sprouts evenly. Measure any pan juices; do not add unmeasured oil.
