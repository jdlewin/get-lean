Steakhouse Sirloin & Veg

Makes 2 servings. About 750-900 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

1.5 lb lean sirloin steaks, trimmedSeason with salt and pepper; sear or grill to preference and rest 5-10 minutes before slicing.
1 lb asparagusTrim woody ends; roast or pan-sear until tender-crisp.
1 medium cauliflowerCut into florets, toss with half the olive oil, and roast at 425F for 25-35 minutes.
4 cups arugulaToss with lemon, Dijon, garlic, and remaining oil as a fresh side.
1 tbsp olive oil, dividedHalf for the cauliflower roast, half for the arugula dressing; measure.
1 tbsp butterFinish the rested steaks with a small amount.
Optional 1 oz blue cheese totalSplit evenly over the two plates, or skip for a lighter plate.
1 lemon + 1 tsp Dijon + 1 garlic clove + salt and pepperDress the arugula; season the steaks.

Process

  1. Roast cauliflower at 425F, 25-35 minutes.
  2. Sear seasoned steaks to preference; rest at least 5-10 minutes, then slice.
  3. Cook asparagus; toss arugula with lemon, Dijon, garlic, and oil.
  4. Finish steaks with butter; plate with vegetables and evenly split blue cheese if using.

Note: Cut, trimming, and butter swing the calories — skip the blue cheese for a lighter plate.
