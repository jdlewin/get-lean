Garlic-Parmesan Chicken & Veg (dinner)

Makes 2 servings. About 650-800 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of operation-get-lean.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breast — Season with salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning; sear to 165F and rest.
  • 1 medium cauliflower — Cut into florets, toss with oil and seasoning, and roast at 425F for 25-35 minutes.
  • 1 lb Brussels sprouts — Halve, toss with oil and seasoning, and roast with the cauliflower.
  • 30 g finely grated Parmesan — Stir into the pan sauce at the end.
  • 1 tbsp olive oil — Toss with the vegetables before roasting; measure.
  • 1 tbsp butter — Melt in the chicken pan for the sauce base.
  • 4 garlic cloves — Mince and cook about 30 seconds in the butter.
  • 1/2 cup low-sodium chicken stock — Add with lemon juice and scrape up the browned bits.
  • Juice of 1/2 lemon + Italian seasoning, salt, pepper — Season the sauce and chicken.

Process

  1. Roast cauliflower and Brussels sprouts at 425F, 25-35 minutes, turning once.
  2. Sear seasoned chicken to 165F; remove to rest.
  3. Cook garlic 30 seconds in butter; add stock and lemon, scraping the pan.
  4. Stir in Parmesan, return chicken to coat, and plate with the vegetables.

Note: Roast the vegetables until browned — flavor without extra sauce.
