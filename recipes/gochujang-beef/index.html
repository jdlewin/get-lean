Gochujang Beef, Cabbage & Spinach (lunch)

Makes 2 servings. About 650-750 kcal, 60-70 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lb 93% lean ground beef — Brown in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up, and drain excess fat.
  • 6 cups thinly sliced green cabbage — Add after the aromatics and cook 4-6 minutes until softened but still a little crisp.
  • 4 packed cups baby spinach — Fold in last and cook just until wilted.
  • 2 large eggs — Cook to your preference in a separate nonstick pan.
  • 3 scallions — Slice thin for topping.
  • 3 garlic cloves + 1 tbsp grated ginger — Mince the garlic and grate the ginger, then cook 30 seconds in the beef fat.
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce — Stir in with the gochujang and vinegar to season the beef.
  • 2 tsp gochujang to start — Measure (don't free-pour); add more only if desired.
  • 2 tsp sesame oil — Drizzle a little over each finished portion.
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar + black pepper — Stir the vinegar into the beef; season with pepper.

Process

  1. Brown the beef, breaking it up; drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Add garlic and ginger for 30 seconds, then stir in soy sauce, gochujang, and rice vinegar.
  3. Add cabbage 4-6 minutes until crisp-tender; fold in spinach until wilted.
  4. Divide into two portions, top each with one egg, scallions, and sesame oil.

Note: Calories hinge on the beef's fat content and whether fat is drained. Keep gochujang mild and measured.
