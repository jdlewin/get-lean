Peanut-Lime Shrimp Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 500-600 kcal, 60-70 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

1.5 lb peeled, deveined shrimpThaw, pat dry, and sear 1-2 minutes per side until opaque.
4 cups thinly sliced cabbageToss with most of the dressing as the slaw base.
1 cucumberCut into matchsticks and toss with the slaw.
4 cups baby spinachToss with the slaw.
2 tbsp natural peanut butterWhisk into the dressing; measure, don't free-pour.
2 tsp sesame oilHeat in the skillet for searing the shrimp.
2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauceWhisk into the dressing.
Juice of 2 limesWhisk into the dressing.
1 tbsp rice vinegar + 2 minced garlic cloves + 1 tsp grated gingerWhisk into the dressing; thin with water a teaspoon at a time.
Optional scallions + sesame seedsSlice/sprinkle over the finished bowls.

Process

  1. Whisk PB, soy, lime, vinegar, garlic, and ginger; thin with water until pourable.
  2. Sear shrimp in hot sesame oil, 1-2 minutes per side.
  3. Toss cabbage, cucumber, and spinach with most of the dressing; top with shrimp.
  4. Finish with remaining dressing and scallions; refrigerate components separately if prepping ahead.

Note: Peanut butter is calorie-dense — measure it. Mild, tangy sauce, not spicy.
