Philly Cheesesteak Skillet

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 65-80 g protein, 25-40 g carbs, 30-45 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Sirloin, lean 1.5 lb
Bell peppers 2
Yellow onion 1 medium
Provolone 4 slices
Olive oil, divided 2 tsp
Broccoli florets 2 cups
Worcestershire sauce 1 tbsp
Garlic powder 1 tsp
Black pepper to taste

Process

  1. Chill the sirloin briefly, then slice thin across the grain; pat dry and season with pepper and garlic powder.
  2. Sear the steak in batches in 1 tsp oil so it browns; set aside on a plate.
  3. Slice the peppers and onion; cook in the remaining oil 6-8 minutes until softened and lightly browned. Steam or microwave the broccoli until tender-crisp.
  4. Return the steak and toss with the Worcestershire. Portion in two; lay two provolone slices over each, cover briefly to melt, and serve with the broccoli.

Note: Use lean trimmed sirloin and measured oil. No bread needed — peppers and onions give the cheesesteak flavor.
