Mediterranean Chicken Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 550-650 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

1.5 lb boneless, skinless chicken breastSeason with oregano, salt, and pepper; grill or sear to 165F, rest, then slice.
4 cups arugula + 4 cups baby spinachDivide between two containers as the salad base.
1 medium cucumberChop and split between the two containers.
1 small apple, diced (optional)Add just before eating so it stays fresh; omit for fewer carbs.
20 g pecansChop and divide between servings.
1 tbsp olive oilWhisk into the dressing.
2 tsp Dijon mustardWhisk into the dressing for bite.
Juice of 1 lemon (or 2 tbsp red-wine vinegar)Whisk into the dressing; add water to thin.
1 garlic clove + oregano, salt, pepperGrate the garlic fine into the dressing; season the chicken with the rest.

Process

  1. Season chicken and cook to 165F; rest, then slice.
  2. Whisk oil, Dijon, lemon, garlic, and pepper (thin with water if needed).
  3. Box the greens, cucumber, chicken, and pecans; keep dressing separate.
  4. Add apple just before eating.

Note: Omit the apple to keep carbs lower — lemon, Dijon, and pecans carry the flavor.
