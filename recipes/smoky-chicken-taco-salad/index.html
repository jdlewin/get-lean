Smoky Chicken Taco Salad

Makes 2 servings. About 600-700 kcal, 65-75 g protein, 20-30 g carbs, 22-30 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Chicken breast, boneless skinless 1.5 lb
Romaine, chopped 4 cups
Baby spinach 2 cups
Tomatoes, chopped 1 cup
Avocado 1 medium
Cheddar, shredded 40 g
Greek yogurt, plain nonfat 1/2 cup
Salsa 1/4 cup, plus extra
Lime juice Juice of 1 lime
Cumin 2 tsp
Smoked paprika 1 tsp
Garlic powder 1 tsp
Salt and pepper to taste

Process

  1. Coat the chicken with the cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper; sear or grill to 165F. Rest and slice.
  2. Squeeze the lime over the yogurt and salsa; stir into a dressing, thinning with water if needed.
  3. Bowl the greens and tomatoes; add the chicken, diced avocado, and cheddar, dividing both evenly between servings.
  4. Serve with the dressing and extra salsa on the side; keep the dressing separate if prepping ahead.

Note: Avocado and cheddar portions drive the calories — measure and divide them evenly.
