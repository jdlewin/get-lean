Layout B — Stations

Same cook, three piles of work. Take one pile each, or run them in order.

Oven — 425°F

  1. Cauliflower in: florets, half the oil, salt, pepper. 25–35 min.
  2. Meatballs in beside it: 12 balls, 15–20 min to 165°F.
  3. Out: meatballs to the sauce, cauliflower to containers.

Stove

  1. Chicken to 165°F, rest, slice.
  2. Sauce simmers 15 min, meatballs in for 5.
  3. Zucchini browned. Dinner split in two.

Board

  1. Three dressings jarred.
  2. Cabbage sliced, boxed dry. Greens boxed, dressing separate.
