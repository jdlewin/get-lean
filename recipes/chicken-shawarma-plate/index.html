Chicken Shawarma Plate with Hummus

Makes 2 servings. About 650-800 kcal, 65-80 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
boneless, skinless chicken breast 1½ lb (680 g)
romaine or mixed greens 6 cups
cucumber, diced 1
tomato, diced 1 large
hummus 4 tbsp
plain nonfat Greek yogurt 1 cup
olive oil 1 tbsp
Juice of 1 lemon
ground cumin 2 tsp
paprika 2 tsp
ground coriander 1 tsp
turmeric ½ tsp
garlic cloves, minced 3
Salt and black pepper

Process

  1. Slice the chicken into thick strips. Toss with the olive oil, cumin, paprika, coriander, turmeric, garlic, half the lemon juice, salt, and pepper.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken in batches until browned and cooked to 165°F / 74°C.
  3. Mix the Greek yogurt with the remaining lemon juice, a pinch of salt, and black pepper.
  4. Divide the greens, cucumber, and tomato between two plates.
  5. Add half the chicken to each plate, along with 2 tbsp hummus and half the yogurt sauce.

Note: Use plain hummus and measure it. The plate is designed to be filling without pita, rice, or flatbread.
