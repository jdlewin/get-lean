Steakhouse Sirloin with Asparagus

Makes 2 servings. About 700-850 kcal, 60-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
lean sirloin steak, trimmed 1½ lb (680 g)
asparagus, woody ends removed 1 lb (454 g)
cauliflower, cut into florets 1 medium
olive oil 1 tbsp
butter 1 tbsp
garlic cloves, minced 2
Dijon mustard 1 tsp
Juice of ½ lemon
Salt, black pepper, and dried thyme

Process

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F / 220°C. Toss the cauliflower with half the olive oil, salt, pepper, and thyme. Roast for 25–35 minutes.
  2. Toss the asparagus with the remaining olive oil and roast on a separate tray for 10–15 minutes, depending on thickness.
  3. Pat the steak dry and season generously with salt and pepper.
  4. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high to high heat. Sear the steak, turning as needed, until it reaches your preferred doneness. Use a thermometer rather than relying on time alone; rest the steak for 5–10 minutes before slicing.
  5. Lower the heat. Add the butter and garlic and cook briefly. Stir in the Dijon and lemon juice, plus a splash of water if needed.
  6. Slice the steak against the grain and divide it evenly between two plates. Serve with the roasted vegetables and pan sauce.

Note: Steak thickness and trimming can change calories considerably. Measure the butter and oil rather than pouring freely.
