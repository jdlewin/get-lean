Smoky Chicken Fajita Bowl over Lettuce

Makes 2 servings. About 550-650 kcal, 65-75 g protein per serving. Part of The Plan; collected in the Get Lean cookbook.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
chicken breast, sliced 1½ lb
bell peppers 3
onion 1 large
olive oil 1 tbsp
smoked paprika 2 tsp
cumin 2 tsp
garlic powder 1 tsp
oregano 1 tsp
Juice of 1 lime; 4 cups romaine; ½ avocado; 2 tbsp nonfat Greek yogurt; salt and pepper

Process

  1. Season chicken with spices, salt and pepper.
  2. Cook chicken in batches with half the oil until 165°F. Use remaining oil to cook peppers and onion until softened and lightly charred.
  3. Return chicken to pan and add lime. Divide romaine between two containers; top with chicken mixture, ¼ avocado and 1 tbsp yogurt each. Keep lettuce separate until serving.
