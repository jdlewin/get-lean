Gochujang Beef, Cabbage & Spinach

Makes 2 servings. About 650-750 kcal, 60-70 g protein, 20-30 g carbs, 35-45 g fat per serving. Part of The Plan.

Ingredients

Ingredient Quantity
Ground beef, 93% lean 1.5 lb
Green cabbage, thinly sliced 6 cups
Baby spinach 4 packed cups
Eggs 2 large
Scallions 3
Garlic cloves 3
Fresh ginger, grated 1 tbsp
Soy sauce, low-sodium 2 tbsp
Gochujang 2 tsp, to start
Sesame oil 2 tsp
Rice vinegar 1 tbsp
Black pepper to taste

Process

  1. Brown the beef in a large skillet over medium-high heat, breaking it up; drain excess fat if needed.
  2. Mince the garlic and grate the ginger; cook 30 seconds in the beef fat. Stir in the soy sauce, measured gochujang, and rice vinegar; season with pepper.
  3. Add the cabbage and cook 4-6 minutes until softened but still a little crisp; fold in the spinach last, just until wilted.
  4. Meanwhile cook the eggs to your preference in a separate nonstick pan. Divide the skillet into two portions; top each with one egg, thin-sliced scallions, and a drizzle of sesame oil.

Note: Calories hinge on the beef's fat content and whether fat is drained. Keep gochujang mild and measured.
